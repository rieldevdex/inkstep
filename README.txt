INKSTEP.SITE

index.html      the whole website (styles and animations are inside it)
favicon.svg     the small icon shown in the browser tab
wrangler.jsonc  how Cloudflare publishes the site (no build step: the files are served as they are)
.assetsignore   files that are not part of the website (this README, wrangler.jsonc)

Fonts load from Google Fonts, so there is nothing else to upload.
Keep index.html and favicon.svg together at the top level of your hosting.

The Eighthundred preview in "What we build" copies one question, its design and its numbers
from eighthundred.site. When Eighthundred's numbers change, update the facts list and the
"Counted on" date next to it.
