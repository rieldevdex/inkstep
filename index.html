What we build

We start from a working product.

Alpha · live now

Eighthundred

A free SAT Math platform named after the perfect score. It teaches step by step, then diagnoses why you got a question wrong and plans practice around it.

This year the club will grow its original question bank, roll out Otto, the AI tutor, to every student with safety rules for under-18 users, test it with students at school, and prepare a public beta.

Meet Otto New · alpha

Eighthundred’s AI tutor sits beside every question. He sees what you answered, gives hints before answers, names the kind of mistake you made, and can set up a similar question in one tap — in English or Vietnamese.

eighthundred.site
  • 24lessons across 8 units, covering all four SAT Math areas, each with an interactive lab and step-by-step solutions
  • 266written questions, plus 72 generators that create new questions without limit
  • 22questions in Real Practice, a two-module Digital SAT-style test in 35 minutes where module 2 adapts to how you did in module 1
  • 3mistake types Eighthundred tells apart: a slip, a concept gap, or not recognising the question type
  • 30free messages a day with Otto, the AI tutor, for every signed-in student

How we work

Every two weeks, we climb one step.

Each cycle ends with at least one change shipped to eighthundred.site.

  1. 01Pick a goalThe team agrees on one thing to ship.
  2. 02DesignScreens, flows and visuals.
  3. 03BuildWrite the content and the code.
  4. 04Content checkTwo members solve every question on their own.
  5. 05Tech checkTest before anything goes live.
  6. 06ShipRelease it to real students.
  7. 07ListenCollect feedback for the next cycle.

Steps 4 and 5 are our two safety nets. Content that isn't right goes back to the Content team, and bugs go back to Developers, before anything is released.

Four events, two open to the whole school.

Question Jam

The best questions go live on Eighthundred with the author's name.

Before each SAT date

SAT Math Week

A week of practice on Eighthundred: a daily challenge, one Real Practice mock test, and a class leaderboard.

Prizes for the most improved student and class.

End of each semester · showcase

Ink & Code Day

Each team shows what it built. Students and teachers try it and give feedback on the spot.

No prizes. Every team gets written feedback.

Semester 2 · competition
