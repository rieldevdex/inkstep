What we build

We start from a working product.

Beta · live now

Eighthundred

A free SAT Math platform named after the perfect score. It teaches step by step, asks what went wrong after every miss, files the question in a Mistake Notebook, and brings it back until you solve it on your own.

Eighthundred is now in public beta (version 0.10, October 2026). This year the club will grow its original question bank, roll out Otto, the AI tutor, to every student with safety rules for under-18 users, and test it with students at school.

Meet Otto Beta

Eighthundred’s AI tutor sits beside every question. He already sees the question, your answer, how sure you were and the worked solution, gives hints before answers, names the Reason behind a mistake and its fix, and replies in English or Vietnamese. Free with a daily message limit; he needs a free account.

eighthundred.site
  • 24lessons in 8 units, covering all four SAT Math areas, each with an interactive lab and a step-by-step walkthrough
  • 314questions with full worked solutions, plus 72 generators that make new-number versions of each skill
  • 44questions in a Real Practice test: a full math section in the Digital SAT's format, two modules of 22 questions and 35 minutes each, where module 2 adapts to how you did in module 1
  • 3Reasons Eighthundred asks about after every miss: a slip, an idea you didn't know yet, or an idea you didn't recognise. Each one gets its own fix
  • 0ads, paywalls or paid plans. Running costs are covered by donations, and every donation and expense is listed publicly

How we work

Every two weeks, we climb one step.

Each cycle ends with at least one change shipped to eighthundred.site.

  1. 01Pick a goalThe team agrees on one thing to ship.
  2. 02DesignScreens, flows and visuals.
  3. 03BuildWrite the content and the code.
  4. 04Content checkTwo members solve every question on their own.
  5. 05Tech checkTest before anything goes live.
  6. 06ShipRelease it to real students.
  7. 07ListenCollect feedback for the next cycle.

Steps 4 and 5 are our two safety nets. Content that isn't right goes back to the Content team, and bugs go back to Developers, before anything is released.

Six teams

Like a small startup, run by students.

Ban Chuyên môn

Content

Writes lessons and original questions, solves them independently, and approves everything before release.

Ban Developer

Developers

Builds new features, fixes bugs, looks after accounts and the database, and tests every release.

Ban Thiết kế

Design

Designs screens, icons, images and video, and keeps the product and the club looking like one brand.

Ban Marketing

Marketing

Brings the product to students through TikTok, Facebook and Instagram, and measures what works.

Ban PR – Đối ngoại

PR & Partnerships

Builds trust with the school, teachers, other clubs and sponsors, and handles the club's emails.

Ban Nhân sự – Hậu cần

People & Logistics

Recruits and trains members, tracks contributions, and prepares rooms and supplies for events.

Four events, two open to the whole school.

Semester 1 · competition

Question Jam

Write an original SAT Math question on a set theme. Judged on accuracy, originality and the quality of the solution.

The best questions go live on Eighthundred with the author's name.

Before each SAT date

SAT Math Week

A week of practice on Eighthundred: a daily challenge, one Real Practice mock test, and a class leaderboard.

Prizes for the most improved student and class.

End of each semester · showcase

Ink & Code Day

Each team shows what it built. Students and teachers try it and give feedback on the spot.

No prizes. Every team gets written feedback.

The best idea may become the club's next project.
